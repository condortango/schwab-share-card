Paste check

Copy a card with the share-card bookmarklet, then press Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on a Mac) anywhere on this page. Whatever image is on your clipboard is drawn below, with its pixel size underneath it: a share card is 1080 by 1920, so those two numbers are how you know the copy carried the card itself.

The image never leaves this tab. It is read out of the paste event and handed to an image element in your own browser; this page uploads nothing, loads nothing and stores nothing.
